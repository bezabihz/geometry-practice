## Part 1 — Points, Lines, Planes, and Angles

---

**Q1. Answer: D — 32**

Set $AM = MB$:
$$3x + 1 = 5x - 7 \Rightarrow 8 = 2x \Rightarrow x = 4$$
$$AM = 3(4)+1 = 13, \quad AB = 2(13) = \boxed{32}$$

**Concept:** Midpoint divides a segment into two equal halves.

---

**Q2. Answer: C — 10**

$$d = \sqrt{(5-(-3))^2 + (-2-4)^2} = \sqrt{64 + 36} = \sqrt{100} = \boxed{10}$$

**Concept:** Distance formula $d = \sqrt{(x_2-x_1)^2 + (y_2-y_1)^2}$

---

**Q3. Answer: B — $(-1, 2)$**

$$M = \left(\frac{2+(-4)}{2}, \frac{-6+10}{2}\right) = \left(\frac{-2}{2}, \frac{4}{2}\right) = \boxed{(-1, 2)}$$

**Concept:** Midpoint formula $M = \left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$

---

**Q4. Answer: A — 65°, 115°, 115°**

Vertical angles to 65° = 65°. Linear pair angles = 180° − 65° = 115°.

$$\boxed{65°,\ 115°,\ 115°}$$

**Concept:** Vertical Angles Theorem + Linear Pair = 180°

---

**Q5. Answer: C — 122.5°**

$$3x + 10 + x + 20 = 180 \Rightarrow 4x + 30 = 180 \Rightarrow x = 37.5$$
$$m\angle A = 3(37.5) + 10 = 112.5 + 10 = \boxed{122.5°}$$

**Concept:** Supplementary angles sum to 180°.

---

**Q6. Answer: C**

The Angle Addition Postulate states: if $D$ is in the interior of $\angle ABC$, then $m\angle ABD + m\angle DBC = m\angle ABC$.

---

**Q7. Answer: C — 13**

$$BC = AC - AB = 20 - 7 = \boxed{13}$$

**Concept:** Segment Addition Postulate: $AB + BC = AC$.

---

**Q8. Answer: B — Postulate 1-1**

Postulate 1-1: Through any two points there is exactly one line.

## Part 2 — Reasoning and Proof

---

**Q9. Answer: C**

Contrapositive negates and reverses both parts:
- Original: "If square, then four right angles"
- Contrapositive: **"If not four right angles, then not a square"**

**Concept:** Contrapositive is logically equivalent to the original conditional.

---

**Q10. Answer: C — Contrapositive**

A conditional and its **contrapositive** always have the same truth value.

---

**Q11. Answer: C — The field is wet**

Law of Detachment: $p \to q$ is true and $p$ is true $\Rightarrow q$ is true.

---

**Q12. Answer: B — Symmetric Property**

If $AB = CD$, then $CD = AB$ — the order is reversed. This is the **Symmetric Property**.

---

**Q13. Answer: C — $p \to r$**

Law of Syllogism: $p\to q$ and $q\to r$ allows us to conclude $p \to r$.

---

**Q14. Answer: C**

A biconditional $p \leftrightarrow q$ is true only when **both** the conditional $p\to q$ **and** its converse $q\to p$ are true.

---

## Part 3 — Parallel and Perpendicular Lines

---

**Q15. Answer: B — 15**

Alternate interior angles are congruent when lines are parallel:
$$4x + 10 = 6x - 20 \Rightarrow 30 = 2x \Rightarrow \boxed{x = 15}$$

---

**Q16. Answer: C — $-\frac{3}{2}$**

Perpendicular slope is the **negative reciprocal**:
$$m_\perp = -\frac{1}{2/3} = -\frac{3}{2}$$

---

**Q17. Answer: B — 28**

Co-interior angles are supplementary when lines are parallel:
$$3x + 15 + 2x + 25 = 180 \Rightarrow 5x + 40 = 180 \Rightarrow x = 28$$

---

**Q18. Answer: B — Converse of the Corresponding Angles Postulate**

To prove lines parallel, use the **converse**: if corresponding angles are congruent → lines parallel.

---

**Q19. Answer: B — 110°**

$\angle 1$ and $\angle 8$ are **alternate exterior angles** → congruent when lines are parallel.
$$m\angle 8 = m\angle 1 = \boxed{110°}$$

---

## Part 4 — Congruent Triangles

---

**Q20. Answer: C — 59°**

$$m\angle C = 180 - 48 - 73 = \boxed{59°}$$

**Concept:** Triangle Angle Sum Theorem.

---

**Q21. Answer: C — SAS**

SAS requires two sides with the **included angle** (the angle between the two sides).

---

**Q22. Answer: B — $EF$**

In $\triangle ABC \cong \triangle DEF$: $BC$ corresponds to $EF$. So $EF = BC = \boxed{15}$.

---

**Q23. Answer: B — 55°**

Exterior Angle Theorem: exterior angle = sum of two non-adjacent interior angles.
$$125 = 70 + m\angle B \Rightarrow m\angle B = \boxed{55°}$$

---

**Q24. Answer: D — AAS**

Two congruent angles + a **non-included** side → **AAS** Congruence Theorem.

---

**Q25. Answer: C — 76°**

Base angles of isosceles triangle: $\angle Q = \angle R = 52°$.
$$m\angle P = 180 - 52 - 52 = \boxed{76°}$$

---

**Q26. Answer: D — HL**

**Hypotenuse-Leg (HL)** applies only to right triangles.

---

**Q27. Answer: C — 60°**

Equilateral triangle is equiangular. Each angle $= \frac{180°}{3} = \boxed{60°}$.

---

## Part 5 — Relationships in Triangles

---

**Q28. Answer: C — 2:1 (vertex to midpoint)**

The **Centroid Theorem**: centroid divides each median in ratio **2:1** from vertex.

---

**Q29. Answer: B — 14**

$$XG = \frac{2}{3}(XM) = \frac{2}{3}(21) = \boxed{14}$$

---

**Q30. Answer: B — No**

Triangle Inequality: $4 + 7 = 11 \not> 12$. The sum must be **strictly greater**.

---

**Q31. Answer: B — $\angle A$ (opposite $BC = 14$)**

The largest angle is opposite the longest side. $BC = 14$ is the longest side, so $\angle A$ is the largest angle.

---

**Q32. Answer: B — Circumcenter**

The **circumcenter** is equidistant from all three vertices (center of circumscribed circle).

---

## Part 6 — Right Triangles and Trigonometry

---

**Q33. Answer: C — 13**

$$c = \sqrt{5^2 + 12^2} = \sqrt{25 + 144} = \sqrt{169} = \boxed{13}$$

This is a **5-12-13 Pythagorean triple**.

---

**Q34. Answer: C — 8**

In a 30-60-90 triangle, the short leg $= \frac{1}{2} \times \text{hypotenuse}$:
$$\text{short leg} = \frac{1}{2}(16) = \boxed{8}$$

---

**Q35. Answer: C — 10.9**

$$AC = \frac{BC}{\sin A} = \frac{7}{\sin 40°} \approx \frac{7}{0.6428} \approx \boxed{10.9}$$

---

**Q36. Answer: B — Cosine**

$$\cos\theta = \frac{\text{adjacent}}{\text{hypotenuse}} \quad \text{(CAH from SOH-CAH-TOA)}$$

---

**Q37. Answer: C — $x\sqrt{2}$**

In a 45-45-90 triangle, hypotenuse $= \text{leg} \times \sqrt{2} = \boxed{x\sqrt{2}}$.

---

## Part 7 — Transformations

---

**Q38. Answer: B — $(-3, -5)$**

Reflection over $y$-axis: $(x, y) \to (-x, y)$:
$$P(3, -5) \to P'(-3, -5)$$

---

**Q39. Answer: C — $(2, 3)$**

Dilation with scale factor $\frac{1}{2}$: $(x, y) \to \left(\frac{x}{2}, \frac{y}{2}\right)$:
$$A(4, 6) \to A'\left(2, 3\right)$$

---

**Q40. Answer: C — $(-5, 2)$**

Rotation 90° CCW about origin: $(x, y) \to (-y, x)$:
$$Q(2, 5) \to Q'(-5, 2)$$
