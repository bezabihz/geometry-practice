## Part 1 — Proportions and Similarity (Ch. 6)

---

**Question 1.**  
$\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF$ with $AB = 6$, $BC = 9$, and $DE = 4$. Find $EF$.

- A) 6
- B) 3
- **C) 6**
- D) 13.5

*(Scale factor $= \frac{AB}{DE} = \frac{6}{4} = \frac{3}{2}$; so $EF = \frac{9 \cdot 2}{3} = 6$)*

---

**Question 2.**  
In $\triangle PQR$, $\overline{ST} \parallel \overline{QR}$ with $S$ on $\overline{PQ}$ and $T$ on $\overline{PR}$. If $PS = 4$, $SQ = 6$, and $PT = 5$, find $TR$.

- A) 5
- B) 6
- **C) 7.5**
- D) 10

*(Triangle Proportionality: $\frac{PS}{SQ} = \frac{PT}{TR}$ → $\frac{4}{6} = \frac{5}{TR}$ → $TR = 7.5$)*

---

**Question 3.**  
The midsegment of a triangle has length 11. What is the length of the side it is parallel to?

- A) 5.5
- **B) 22**
- C) 11
- D) 33

---

**Question 4.**  
Two triangles have two pairs of congruent angles. Which similarity shortcut applies?

- **A) AA**
- B) SSS similarity

- D) HL

---

**Question 5.**  
$\triangle ABC \sim \triangle XYZ$ with scale factor $\dfrac{3}{5}$. If the area of $\triangle XYZ = 75$, find the area of $\triangle ABC$.

- **A) 27**
- B) 45
- C) 125
- D) 15

*(Area ratio $= k^2 = \frac{9}{25}$; Area of $\triangle ABC = \frac{9}{25}(75) = 27$)*

---

**Question 6.**  
In $\triangle ABC$, $\overrightarrow{AD}$ bisects $\angle A$ with $D$ on $\overline{BC}$. If $AB = 10$, $AC = 6$, and $BD = 5$, find $DC$.

- A) 2
- B) 4
- **C) 3**
- D) 8

*(Angle Bisector: $\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{AC} = \frac{10}{6} = \frac{5}{3}$; so $DC = 3$)*

---

**Question 7.**

- **C) $\frac{9}{25}$**
- D) $\frac{27}{125}$

## Part 5 — Surface Area (Ch. 12)

---

**Question 28.**  
A rectangular prism has dimensions $3 \times 4 \times 5$. Find its surface area.

- A) 60
- **B) 94**
- C) 47
- D) 120

*(SA $= 2(3\cdot4 + 4\cdot5 + 3\cdot5) = 2(12+20+15) = 2(47) = 94$)*

---

**Question 29.**  
A cylinder has radius 5 and height 8. Find its **lateral area**.

- A) $40\pi$
- **B) $80\pi$**
- C) $90\pi$
- D) $130\pi$

*(LA $= 2\pi rh = 2\pi(5)(8) = 80\pi$)*

---

**Question 30.**  
A cone has radius 6 and slant height 10. Find its total surface area.

- A) $60\pi$
- B) $96\pi$
- **C) $96\pi$**
- D) $156\pi$

*(SA $= \pi r\ell + \pi r^2 = \pi(6)(10) + \pi(36) = 60\pi + 36\pi = 96\pi$)*

---

**Question 31.**  
A square pyramid has base side 8 and slant height 5. Find the **lateral area**.

- A) 40

- **C) 80**
- D) 160

## Part 7 — Probability and Similar Solids

---

**Question 38.**  
Two similar cylinders have radii 2 and 6. What is the ratio of their volumes?

- A) $\frac{1}{3}$
- B) $\frac{1}{9}$
- **C) $\frac{1}{27}$**
- D) $\frac{2}{6}$

*(Scale factor $= \frac{1}{3}$; Volume ratio $= \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^3 = \frac{1}{27}$)*

---

**Question 39.**  
A dartboard is a circle of radius 12. A smaller circle of radius 4 is the bullseye. What is the probability of hitting the bullseye?

- A) $\frac{1}{4}$
- B) $\frac{1}{6}$
- **C) $\frac{1}{9}$**
- D) $\frac{4}{12}$

*(P $= \frac{\pi(4)^2}{\pi(12)^2} = \frac{16}{144} = \frac{1}{9}$)*

---

**Question 40.**  
A rectangular room is 15 ft × 20 ft. A circular rug of radius 5 ft is placed inside. What is the probability a dropped object lands on the rug?

- A) $\frac{\pi}{12}$
- **B) $\frac{5\pi}{12} \cdot \frac{1}{5} = \frac{\pi}{12}$**
- C) $\frac{25\pi}{300}$
- D) Both A and C are correct

*(P $= \frac{25\pi}{300} = \frac{\pi}{12} \approx 26.2\%$)*
