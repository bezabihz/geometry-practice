## Part 1 — Proportions and Similarity

---

**Q1. Answer: C — 6**

Scale factor: $k = \frac{AB}{DE} = \frac{6}{4} = \frac{3}{2}$

$$EF = \frac{BC}{k} = \frac{9}{3/2} = \frac{9 \cdot 2}{3} = \boxed{6}$$

**Concept:** Corresponding sides of similar triangles are proportional.

---

**Q2. Answer: C — 7.5**

Triangle Proportionality Theorem: $\frac{PS}{SQ} = \frac{PT}{TR}$

$$\frac{4}{6} = \frac{5}{TR} \Rightarrow TR = \frac{6 \times 5}{4} = \boxed{7.5}$$

---

**Q3. Answer: B — 22**

Midsegment $= \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base}$, so base $= 2 \times \text{midsegment}$:
$$\text{base} = 2 \times 11 = \boxed{22}$$

**Concept:** Triangle Midsegment Theorem (Theorem 6-5).

---

**Q4. Answer: A — AA**

**Angle-Angle (AA) Similarity Postulate**: two pairs of congruent angles → similar triangles.

---

**Q5. Answer: A — 27**

Area ratio $= k^2 = \left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^2 = \frac{9}{25}$

$$\text{Area of } \triangle ABC = \frac{9}{25} \times 75 = \boxed{27}$$

---

**Q6. Answer: C — 3**

Angle Bisector Proportionality Theorem: $\frac{BD}{DC} = \frac{AB}{AC}$

$$\frac{5}{DC} = \frac{10}{6} \Rightarrow DC = \frac{5 \times 6}{10} = \boxed{3}$$

---

**Q7. Answer: C — $\frac{9}{25}$**

Perimeter ratio $= k = \frac{15}{25} = \frac{3}{5}$

Area ratio $= k^2 = \left(\frac{3}{5}\right)^2 = \boxed{\frac{9}{25}}$

## Part 2 — Quadrilaterals

---

**Q8. Answer: B — 68°**

In a parallelogram, **opposite angles are congruent**:
$$m\angle C = m\angle A = \boxed{68°}$$

---

**Q9. Answer: C — 28**

Diagonals of a parallelogram bisect each other → $AE = CE$:
$$3x + 2 = 5x - 6 \Rightarrow 2x = 8 \Rightarrow x = 4$$
$$AE = 3(4) + 2 = 14, \quad AC = 2 \times 14 = \boxed{28}$$

---

**Q10. Answer: D — Square**

A **square** has diagonals that are both congruent (rectangle property) AND perpendicular (rhombus property).

---

**Q11. Answer: B — 50**

$$A = \frac{1}{2}h(b_1 + b_2) = \frac{1}{2}(5)(7 + 13) = \frac{1}{2}(5)(20) = \boxed{50}$$

---

**Q12. Answer: C — 12**

$$\text{midsegment} = \frac{b_1 + b_2}{2} \Rightarrow 9 = \frac{6 + b_2}{2} \Rightarrow b_2 = \boxed{12}$$

---

**Q13. Answer: B — 118°**

In a trapezoid, co-interior angles on the same leg are supplementary:
$$180° - 62° = \boxed{118°}$$

---

## Part 3 — Circles

---

**Q14. Answer: B — 80°**

A **central angle** equals its intercepted arc:
$$m\overset{\frown}{arc} = m\angle_{\text{central}} = \boxed{80°}$$

---

**Q15. Answer: B — 62°**

Inscribed Angle Theorem: $m\angle = \frac{1}{2}m\overset{\frown}{arc}$:
$$m\angle = \frac{1}{2}(124°) = \boxed{62°}$$

---

**Q16. Answer: B — 97°**

Inscribed quadrilateral: opposite angles are supplementary:
$$m\angle C = 180° - 83° = \boxed{97°}$$

---

**Q17. Answer: C — 70°**

Interior angle formed by two chords:
$$m\angle = \frac{1}{2}(60° + 80°) = \frac{140°}{2} = \boxed{70°}$$

---

**Q18. Answer: A — 60°**

External angle (tangent-secant):
$$m\angle = \frac{1}{2}(\text{far arc} - \text{near arc}) = \frac{1}{2}(200° - 80°) = \frac{120°}{2} = \boxed{60°}$$

---

**Q19. Answer: C — 6**

Chord-Chord Segments: product of segments are equal:
$$4 \times 9 = 6 \times x \Rightarrow x = \frac{36}{6} = \boxed{6}$$

---

**Q20. Answer: C — 16**

Tangent-Secant from external point: $t^2 = \text{external} \times \text{whole}$:
$$8^2 = 4 \times \text{whole} \Rightarrow \text{whole} = \frac{64}{4} = \boxed{16}$$

---

**Q21. Answer: C — 90°**

Corollary 10-2: An angle inscribed in a semicircle always measures **90°** (right angle).

## Part 4 — Areas of Polygons and Circles

---

**Q22. Answer: B — 66**

$$A = \frac{1}{2}h(b_1 + b_2) = \frac{1}{2}(6)(8 + 14) = 3(22) = \boxed{66}$$

---

**Q23. Answer: C — $81\pi$**

$$A = \pi r^2 = \pi(9)^2 = \boxed{81\pi}$$

---

**Q24. Answer: B — $25\pi$**

$$A_{\text{sector}} = \frac{90}{360}\pi r^2 = \frac{1}{4}\pi(100) = \boxed{25\pi}$$

---

**Q25. Answer: B — 80**

$$A = \frac{1}{2}d_1 d_2 = \frac{1}{2}(10)(16) = \boxed{80}$$

---

**Q26. Answer: C — $54\sqrt{3}$**

$$A = \frac{1}{2}Pa = \frac{1}{2}(6 \times 6)(3\sqrt{3}) = \frac{1}{2}(36)(3\sqrt{3}) = \boxed{54\sqrt{3}}$$

---

**Q27. Answer: D — Both A and C are correct**

$$P = \frac{\pi(4)^2}{12^2} = \frac{16\pi}{144} = \frac{\pi}{9}$$

Both option A ($\frac{\pi}{9}$) and option C ($\frac{16\pi}{144}$) equal the same value.

## Part 5 — Surface Area

---

**Q28. Answer: B — 94**

$$SA = 2(lw + lh + wh) = 2(3\cdot4 + 3\cdot5 + 4\cdot5) = 2(12+15+20) = 2(47) = \boxed{94}$$

---

**Q29. Answer: B — $80\pi$**

$$LA = 2\pi rh = 2\pi(5)(8) = \boxed{80\pi}$$

---

**Q30. Answer: C — $96\pi$**

$$SA = \pi r\ell + \pi r^2 = \pi(6)(10) + \pi(36) = 60\pi + 36\pi = \boxed{96\pi}$$

---

**Q31. Answer: C — 80**

$$LA = \frac{1}{2}P\ell = \frac{1}{2}(4 \times 8)(5) = \frac{1}{2}(32)(5) = \boxed{80}$$

---

**Q32. Answer: C — $196\pi$**

$$SA = 4\pi r^2 = 4\pi(7)^2 = 4\pi(49) = \boxed{196\pi}$$

---

## Part 6 — Volume

---

**Q33. Answer: C — 216**

$$V = lwh = 4 \times 6 \times 9 = \boxed{216}$$

---

**Q34. Answer: C — $90\pi$**

$$V = \pi r^2 h = \pi(3)^2(10) = 9\pi(10) = \boxed{90\pi}$$

---

**Q35. Answer: B — 96**

$$V = \frac{1}{3}Bh = \frac{1}{3}(6^2)(8) = \frac{1}{3}(36)(8) = \frac{288}{3} = \boxed{96}$$

---

**Q36. Answer: C — $48\pi$**

$$V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{3}\pi(4)^2(9) = \frac{1}{3}\pi(16)(9) = \frac{144\pi}{3} = \boxed{48\pi}$$

---

**Q37. Answer: A — $36\pi$**

$$V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi(3)^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi(27) = \frac{108\pi}{3} = \boxed{36\pi}$$

---

## Part 7 — Probability and Similar Solids

---

**Q38. Answer: C — $\frac{1}{27}$**

Scale factor $k = \frac{2}{6} = \frac{1}{3}$; Volume ratio $= k^3 = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^3 = \boxed{\frac{1}{27}}$

---

**Q39. Answer: C — $\frac{1}{9}$**

$$P = \frac{\text{Area of bullseye}}{\text{Area of dartboard}} = \frac{\pi(4)^2}{\pi(12)^2} = \frac{16}{144} = \boxed{\frac{1}{9}}$$

---

**Q40. Answer: D — Both A and C are correct**

$$P = \frac{\pi(5)^2}{15 \times 20} = \frac{25\pi}{300} = \frac{\pi}{12}$$

Options A and C both simplify to $\frac{\pi}{12} \approx 26.2\%$.
