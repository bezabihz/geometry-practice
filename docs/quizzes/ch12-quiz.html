Question 2 of 8
The lateral surface area of a cylinder with radius 4 and height 10 is:
B is correct. Lateral SA of cylinder = 2πrh = 2π(4)(10) = 80π. (Total SA adds two bases: 80π + 2π(16) = 112π.)
Question 3 of 8
A square pyramid has a base side of 6 and slant height of 5. What is the lateral surface area?
B is correct. Lateral SA of pyramid = ½Pl = ½(4×6)(5) = ½(24)(5) = 60. (P = perimeter of base, l = slant height.)
