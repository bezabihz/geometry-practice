Question 1 of 8
In a parallelogram, opposite angles are:
C is correct. In a parallelogram, opposite angles are congruent, and consecutive angles are supplementary (sum to 180°).
Question 2 of 8
Which property is TRUE for a rectangle but NOT necessarily for a general parallelogram?
C is correct. In a rectangle, the diagonals are congruent (equal length). In a general parallelogram, diagonals bisect each other but are not necessarily equal.
Question 3 of 8
Which quadrilateral has diagonals that are perpendicular bisectors of each other?
C is correct. In a rhombus, the diagonals are perpendicular bisectors of each other. A square (rhombus + rectangle) has this property too, plus congruent diagonals.
Question 4 of 8
The midsegment of a trapezoid equals:
C is correct. Trapezoid Midsegment Theorem: the midsegment = (b₁ + b₂)/2, and it is parallel to both bases.
Question 5 of 8
A square is ALWAYS a:
C is correct. A square has all right angles (→ rectangle) AND all equal sides (→ rhombus). It is the most specific type of parallelogram.
Question 6 of 8
In parallelogram ABCD, AB = 2x + 3 and CD = 4x − 5. Find x.
B is correct. Opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal: 2x+3 = 4x−5 → 8 = 2x → x = 4.
Question 7 of 8
A kite has exactly one pair of congruent opposite angles. Where are those angles?
B is correct. In a kite, the two angles between the non-congruent pairs of sides (the "side" angles) are congruent. The vertex angles (where equal sides meet) are generally not equal to each other.
Question 8 of 8
An isosceles trapezoid has base angles of:
B is correct. Isosceles Trapezoid: base angles are congruent (both angles at the same base are equal). The diagonals are also congruent in an isosceles trapezoid.
