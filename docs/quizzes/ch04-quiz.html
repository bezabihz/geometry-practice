Question 1 of 8
Which congruence postulate requires knowing all three pairs of sides are congruent?
C is correct. SSS (Side-Side-Side): if three sides of one triangle are congruent to three sides of another, the triangles are congruent.
Question 2 of 8
CPCTC stands for:
A is correct. CPCTC is used as a reason after proving triangles congruent — it states that ALL corresponding parts (sides and angles) of congruent triangles must also be congruent.
Question 3 of 8
An isosceles triangle has a vertex angle of 50°. What is each base angle?
B is correct. The two base angles are equal. Total = 180°, so each base angle = (180° − 50°)/2 = 65°. Isosceles Base Angles Theorem: base angles of an isosceles triangle are congruent.
Question 4 of 8
The HL (Hypotenuse-Leg) theorem applies only to:
C is correct. HL only works for right triangles. If the hypotenuse and one leg of one right triangle equal the hypotenuse and one leg of another, the triangles are congruent.
Question 5 of 8
In △ABC ≅ △DEF, which angle corresponds to ∠B?
B is correct. The order of vertices in the congruence statement tells you correspondences: A↔D, B↔E, C↔F. So ∠B corresponds to ∠E.
Question 6 of 8
The exterior angle of a triangle equals:
B is correct. Exterior Angle Theorem: an exterior angle = sum of the two remote (non-adjacent) interior angles. E.g., if remote angles are 45° and 70°, exterior angle = 115°.
Question 7 of 8
SAS requires two sides and the included angle. "Included angle" means:
C is correct. The included angle is the one sandwiched between the two known sides. SSA (angle not included) does NOT guarantee congruence — it's the ambiguous case.
Question 8 of 8
Two triangles have two pairs of congruent angles and the non-included side congruent. Which postulate proves them congruent?
D is correct. AAS (Angle-Angle-Side): two angles and a non-included side. (ASA uses two angles and the included side between them.)
