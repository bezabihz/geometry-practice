Question 1 of 8
△ABC ~ △DEF with AB = 6 and DE = 9. If BC = 8, find EF.
B is correct. Scale factor = DE/AB = 9/6 = 3/2. EF = BC × (3/2) = 8 × 1.5 = 12.
Question 2 of 8
Two triangles are similar if they have:
B is correct. AA (Angle-Angle) Similarity: if two angles of one triangle equal two angles of another, the triangles are similar. (Since the third angles must also be equal by the Triangle Angle Sum.)
Question 3 of 8
Solve the proportion: x/12 = 5/8
B is correct. Cross-multiply: 8x = 60 → x = 60/8 = 7.5.
Question 4 of 8
The Triangle Proportionality Theorem states that if a line is parallel to one side of a triangle and intersects the other two sides, it divides those sides:
B is correct. Triangle Proportionality Theorem: a line || to one side divides the other two sides proportionally. E.g., if DE || BC, then AD/DB = AE/EC.
Question 5 of 8
If △PQR ~ △XYZ and the ratio of similarity is 3:1, what is the ratio of their perimeters?
B is correct. Perimeters of similar figures scale by the same ratio as the sides (k = 3:1). Areas scale by k² = 9:1.
Question 6 of 8
The geometric mean of 4 and 9 is:
B is correct. Geometric mean of a and b = √(ab). √(4 × 9) = √36 = 6.
Question 7 of 8
Which similarity shortcut compares all three pairs of sides as ratios?
C is correct. SSS Similarity: if all three pairs of sides are proportional, the triangles are similar. (SAS ~ uses two sides proportional and the included angle congruent.)
Question 8 of 8
A map uses a scale of 1 cm : 50 km. If two cities are 3.5 cm apart on the map, what is the actual distance?
C is correct. 3.5 cm × 50 km/cm = 175 km. This is a direct application of similar figures / scale factors.
