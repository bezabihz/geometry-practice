Question 1 of 8
A right triangle has legs 5 and 12. What is the hypotenuse?
B is correct. Pythagorean Theorem: c² = 5² + 12² = 25 + 144 = 169. c = √169 = 13. (5-12-13 is a Pythagorean triple.)
Question 2 of 8
In a 30-60-90 triangle, if the short leg is 7, what is the hypotenuse?
B is correct. 30-60-90 ratios: short leg : long leg : hypotenuse = 1 : √3 : 2. Hypotenuse = 2 × short leg = 2 × 7 = 14.
Question 3 of 8
In a 45-45-90 triangle with leg = 8, the hypotenuse is:
C is correct. 45-45-90 ratios: leg : leg : hypotenuse = 1 : 1 : √2. Hypotenuse = 8√2.
Question 4 of 8
sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse. If opposite = 6 and hypotenuse = 10, what is sin θ?
C is correct. SOH: sin = Opposite/Hypotenuse = 6/10 = 0.6. (Note: this is a 6-8-10 right triangle, a scaled 3-4-5.)
Question 5 of 8
Which ratio gives the tangent of an angle in a right triangle?
C is correct. TOA: Tangent = Opposite/Adjacent. Memory aid: SOH-CAH-TOA (Sin=O/H, Cos=A/H, Tan=O/A).
Question 6 of 8
A 12-foot ladder leans against a wall at 60° from the ground. How high does it reach? (Leave in exact form.)
B is correct. height = hypotenuse × sin(60°) = 12 × (√3/2) = 6√3 ≈ 10.4 ft.
Question 7 of 8
The altitude from the right angle to the hypotenuse of a right triangle creates two smaller triangles. These triangles are:
B is correct. Geometric Mean (Altitude) Theorem: the altitude to the hypotenuse creates two triangles each similar to the original and to each other. This gives us the geometric mean relationships.
Question 8 of 8
To find a missing angle when you know opposite and adjacent sides, you use:
C is correct. Use the inverse trig function: θ = tan⁻¹(opposite/adjacent). Inverse functions "undo" the ratio to give the angle.
