Question 1 of 8
A transversal crosses two parallel lines. If one angle measures 110°, what is its alternate interior angle?
B is correct. Alternate Interior Angles Theorem: when lines are parallel, alternate interior angles are congruent (equal). So both measure 110°.
Question 2 of 8
Co-interior (same-side interior) angles formed by a transversal cutting parallel lines are:
C is correct. Consecutive (same-side) interior angles are supplementary — they add to 180°. This is the Co-Interior Angles Theorem.
Question 3 of 8
What is the slope of a line parallel to y = 3x − 5?
D is correct. Parallel lines have the same slope. The slope of y = 3x − 5 is 3, so any parallel line also has slope 3.
Question 4 of 8
What is the slope of a line perpendicular to a line with slope 4?
D is correct. Perpendicular slopes are negative reciprocals: m₁ × m₂ = −1. So the perpendicular slope is −1/4.
Question 5 of 8
Corresponding angles formed by a transversal cutting parallel lines are:
C is correct. Corresponding Angles Postulate: when a transversal cuts parallel lines, corresponding angles (same position at each intersection) are congruent.
Question 6 of 8
Two lines cut by a transversal. If alternate exterior angles are congruent, the lines are:
B is correct. Converse of the Alternate Exterior Angles Theorem: if alternate exterior angles are congruent, the lines are parallel. This is one of several ways to prove lines parallel.
Question 7 of 8
Lines that do not intersect and are not in the same plane are called:
C is correct. Skew lines are non-coplanar and non-intersecting. Example: the top edge of a box and a front-to-back edge on the opposite side.
Question 8 of 8
A transversal forms a 72° angle with one parallel line. What is the co-interior (same-side interior) angle at the other parallel line?
C is correct. Co-interior angles are supplementary: 180° − 72° = 108°.
