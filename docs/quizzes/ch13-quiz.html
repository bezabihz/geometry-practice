Question 1 of 8
A rectangular prism has length 6, width 4, height 5. What is the volume?
B is correct. V = lwh = 6 × 4 × 5 = 120.
Question 2 of 8
A cylinder has radius 3 and height 10. What is the volume? (Leave in terms of π.)
C is correct. V = πr²h = π(9)(10) = 90π.
Question 3 of 8
A pyramid has a square base of side 6 and height 8. What is the volume?
B is correct. V = ⅓Bh = ⅓(6²)(8) = ⅓(36)(8) = ⅓(288) = 96.
Question 4 of 8
A cone has radius 4 and height 9. What is the volume? (Leave in terms of π.)
A is correct. V = ⅓πr²h = ⅓π(16)(9) = ⅓(144π) = 48π.
Question 5 of 8
A sphere has radius 3. What is the volume? (Leave in terms of π.)
B is correct. V = (4/3)πr³ = (4/3)π(27) = 36π.
Question 6 of 8
Two similar solids have a length ratio of 1:3. What is the ratio of their volumes?
C is correct. Similar solids with scale factor k have volume ratio k³. Here k = 3, so volume ratio = 3³ = 27 → ratio is 1:27.
Question 7 of 8
Cavalieri's Principle states that two solids with the same height and equal cross-sectional areas at every level have:
B is correct. Cavalieri's Principle: if two solids have equal heights and equal cross-sectional areas at every corresponding level, they have the same volume (even if their shapes differ).
Question 8 of 8
A composite solid is formed by a cylinder (r=3, h=10) with a hemisphere (r=3) on top. What is the total volume? (In terms of π.)
A is correct. Cylinder: π(9)(10) = 90π. Hemisphere = ½ sphere = ½(4/3)π(27) = ½(36π) = 18π. Total = 108π.
