Question 1 of 8
A central angle of a circle measures 80°. What is the measure of its intercepted arc?
C is correct. A central angle equals the measure of its intercepted arc. Both measure 80°.
Question 2 of 8
An inscribed angle intercepts an arc of 100°. What is the inscribed angle?
C is correct. Inscribed Angle Theorem: inscribed angle = ½ × intercepted arc. ½ × 100° = 50°.
Question 3 of 8
A tangent line to a circle at point P is:
B is correct. A tangent is perpendicular to the radius drawn to the point of tangency. This is a key theorem used in many circle problems.
Question 4 of 8
Two tangent segments are drawn from an external point to a circle. They are:
C is correct. Two tangent segments from the same external point are congruent (equal length). This follows from the HL theorem applied to the two right triangles formed.
Question 5 of 8
Two chords intersect inside a circle. Chord AB is cut into segments of 4 and 6. Chord CD is cut into segments of 3 and x. Find x.
C is correct. Chord-Chord product rule: segment₁ × segment₂ = segment₃ × segment₄. So 4 × 6 = 3 × x → 24 = 3x → x = 8.
Question 6 of 8
An inscribed angle that intercepts a semicircle (arc = 180°) measures:
B is correct. Thales' Theorem: an inscribed angle intercepting a semicircle = ½ × 180° = 90°. Any angle inscribed in a semicircle is a right angle.
Question 7 of 8
A secant from an external point creates far arc = 200° and near arc = 80°. The angle at the external point is:
B is correct. Secant-Secant angle from external point = ½(far arc − near arc) = ½(200° − 80°) = ½(120°) = 60°.
Question 8 of 8
The diameter of a circle is a chord that:
C is correct. The diameter passes through the center and is the longest chord of the circle. d = 2r.
