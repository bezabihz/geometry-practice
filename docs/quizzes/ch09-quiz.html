Question 1 of 8
A translation moves every point of a figure:
A is correct. A translation slides every point the same distance in the same direction. It is written as (x, y) → (x + a, y + b).
Question 2 of 8
Point A(3, −2) is reflected across the x-axis. What are the coordinates of A'?
B is correct. Reflection across x-axis: (x, y) → (x, −y). So (3, −2) → (3, 2).
Question 3 of 8
Point B(4, 1) is rotated 90° counterclockwise about the origin. What are the coordinates of B'?
A is correct. 90° CCW rotation: (x, y) → (−y, x). So (4, 1) → (−1, 4).
Question 4 of 8
Which transformation changes the SIZE of a figure?
D is correct. Dilation changes size (scale factor k ≠ 1). Translations, reflections, and rotations are isometries — they preserve both shape and size.
Question 5 of 8
Point C(−2, 5) is reflected across the y-axis. What are the coordinates of C'?
C is correct. Reflection across y-axis: (x, y) → (−x, y). So (−2, 5) → (2, 5).
Question 6 of 8
A dilation with center O and scale factor k = 3 maps point P(2, 4) to:
C is correct. Dilation from origin: (x, y) → (kx, ky) = (3×2, 3×4) = (6, 12).
Question 7 of 8
Point D(1, 3) is rotated 180° about the origin. What are the coordinates of D'?
D is correct. 180° rotation: (x, y) → (−x, −y). So (1, 3) → (−1, −3).
Question 8 of 8
A composition of two reflections across parallel lines is equivalent to a:
B is correct. Two reflections over parallel lines = a translation (the distance is twice the distance between the lines). Two reflections over intersecting lines = a rotation.
