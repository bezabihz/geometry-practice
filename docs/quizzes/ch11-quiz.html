Question 1 of 8
A trapezoid has bases of 6 and 14, and height 5. What is the area?
B is correct. A = ½h(b₁+b₂) = ½(5)(6+14) = ½(5)(20) = 50.
Question 2 of 8
A rhombus has diagonals of 10 and 16. What is the area?
B is correct. A = ½d₁d₂ = ½(10)(16) = 80.
Question 3 of 8
A circle has radius 7. What is the area? (Leave answer in terms of π.)
B is correct. A = πr² = π(7²) = 49π.
Question 4 of 8
A sector of a circle has radius 10 and central angle 90°. What is the sector's area?
B is correct. A = (x/360)πr² = (90/360)π(100) = ¼(100π) = 25π.
Question 5 of 8
A regular hexagon has a perimeter of 48 and apothem of 4√3. What is the area?
A is correct. A = ½Pa = ½(48)(4√3) = ½(192√3) = 96√3.
Question 6 of 8
The height in a triangle's area formula must be:
C is correct. The height MUST be perpendicular to the base, not the slant side. This applies to parallelograms, triangles, and trapezoids.
Question 7 of 8
The arc length of a 60° arc in a circle with radius 12 is:
B is correct. Arc length ℓ = (x/360)(2πr) = (60/360)(2π·12) = (1/6)(24π) = 4π.
Question 8 of 8
A parallelogram has base 9 and height 6. What is the area?
C is correct. A = bh = 9 × 6 = 54. (Not ½bh — that's for triangles.)
