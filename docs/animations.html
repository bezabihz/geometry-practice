Core Concepts Visualized

8 key geometry ideas with step-by-step CSS animations. Watch each loop to build intuition.

1 · Vertical Angles

Opposite angle pairs are always congruent. Watch each pair flash together.

2 · Parallel Lines & Transversal

Orange = alternate interior angles (equal). Green = corresponding angles (equal).

3 · Pythagorean Theorem

The three squares appear in order: leg a², leg b², hypotenuse c². Then a²+b²=c².

4 · Centroid 2:1 Ratio

The dot travels along a median and stops at the centroid — ⅔ from vertex to midpoint.

5 · Inscribed Angle Theorem

The arc sweeps in, then the labels appear: inscribed angle = ½ × central angle.

6 · Reflection across y-axis

Pre-image fades; its mirror image across the y-axis appears.

7 · Dilation from Origin

The small triangle dilates outward from the origin with scale factor k = 2.

8 · Area = ½bh

A triangle is exactly half a parallelogram with the same base and height.
