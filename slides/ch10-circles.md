# Chapter 10
# Circles

### Unit 3 — Quadrilaterals and Circles
### McGraw Hill Glencoe Geometry Texas, 2015

---

## Key Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Circle** | Set of all points equidistant from a center |
| **Radius** | Segment from center to a point on circle |
| **Chord** | Segment with both endpoints on the circle |
| **Diameter** | Chord through the center |
| **Secant** | Line intersecting a circle at two points |
| **Tangent** | Line touching a circle at exactly one point |
| **Arc** | Part of a circle |
| **Central angle** | Angle whose vertex is the center |
| **Inscribed angle** | Angle whose vertex is on the circle |

---

## Arc Measures

| Arc Type | Symbol | Measure |
|----------|--------|---------|
| **Minor arc** | $\overset{\frown}{AB}$ | = central angle |
| **Major arc** | $\overset{\frown}{AXB}$ | $= 360° -$ minor arc |
| **Semicircle** | — | $= 180°$ |

**Arc Addition:** $m\overset{\frown}{ABC} = m\overset{\frown}{AB} + m\overset{\frown}{BC}$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 10-1: Tangent-Radius Perpendicularity

> A **tangent** to a circle is **perpendicular** to the radius drawn to the point of tangency.

$$\overline{OP} \perp \text{tangent at } P$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 10-2: Two Tangents from External Point

> Two **tangent segments** from the same external point are **congruent**.

$$PA = PB \quad (P \text{ external, } A \text{ and } B \text{ tangency points})$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 10-3: Inscribed Angle Theorem

> An **inscribed angle** measures **half** its intercepted arc.

$$m\angle ABC = \frac{1}{2} m\overset{\frown}{AC}$$

*The vertex is on the circle; the sides are chords.*

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Corollary 10-1: Inscribed Angles — Same Arc

> Two **inscribed angles** that intercept the **same arc** are **congruent**.

$$m\overset{\frown}{AC} \text{ same} \Rightarrow \angle ABC \cong \angle ADC$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Corollary 10-2: Inscribed Angle in Semicircle

> An angle **inscribed in a semicircle** is a **right angle**.

$$\text{If } \overline{AC} \text{ is a diameter} \Rightarrow \angle ABC = 90°$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 10-4: Inscribed Quadrilateral

> A quadrilateral can be **inscribed in a circle** if and only if its **opposite angles are supplementary**.

$$m\angle A + m\angle C = 180° \quad \text{and} \quad m\angle B + m\angle D = 180°$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 10-5: Tangent-Chord Angle

> The measure of an angle formed by a **tangent and a chord** equals half the intercepted arc.

$$m\angle = \frac{1}{2} m\overset{\frown}{(\text{intercepted arc})}$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 10-6: Two Chords — Interior Angle

> Two **chords** intersecting **inside** a circle: the angle equals half the sum of the intercepted arcs.

$$m\angle 1 = \frac{1}{2}(m\overset{\frown}{AC} + m\overset{\frown}{BD})$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 10-7: Two Secants/Tangents — Exterior Angle

> Two **secants**, a **secant and a tangent**, or two **tangents** from an external point: the angle equals half the positive difference of the intercepted arcs.

$$m\angle P = \frac{1}{2}(m\overset{\frown}{\text{far}} - m\overset{\frown}{\text{near}})$$

---

## Angle Formed By Summary

| Position | Formula |
|----------|---------|
| Center (central $\angle$) | $m\angle = m\overset{\frown}{\text{arc}}$ |
| On circle (inscribed) | $m\angle = \frac{1}{2}m\overset{\frown}{\text{arc}}$ |
| Inside circle (2 chords) | $m\angle = \frac{1}{2}(\overset{\frown}{\text{arc}_1} + \overset{\frown}{\text{arc}_2})$ |
| Outside circle | $m\angle = \frac{1}{2}(\overset{\frown}{\text{far}} - \overset{\frown}{\text{near}})$ |

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 10-8: Two Chords — Segments

> When two **chords** intersect inside a circle:

$$EA \cdot EB = EC \cdot ED$$

*(Products of chord segments are equal.)*

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 10-9: Two Secants from External Point

> Two **secants** from an external point $P$:

$$PA \cdot PB = PC \cdot PD$$

*(whole secant × external segment)*

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 10-10: Secant and Tangent from External Point

> A **secant** and a **tangent** from external point $P$ (tangent length $= t$):

$$t^2 = PA \cdot PB$$

*(tangent² = whole secant × external segment)*

---

<!-- _class: example -->

### Example — Inscribed Angle

If $m\overset{\frown}{AC} = 80°$, find the inscribed angle $\angle ABC$.

$$m\angle ABC = \frac{1}{2}(80°) = 40°$$
