<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Postulate 11-1: Area Addition

> The area of a region equals the **sum of the areas** of its non-overlapping parts.

$$A_{\text{total}} = A_1 + A_2 + \cdots + A_n$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 11-1: Area of a Parallelogram

> The area of a **parallelogram** equals the **base times the height**.

$$A = bh$$

*The height $h$ is perpendicular to the base — not the slant side!*

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 11-2: Area of a Triangle

> The area of a **triangle** equals **half the base times the height**.

$$A = \frac{1}{2}bh$$

**Alternative (Heron's Formula):**

$$A = \sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}, \quad s = \frac{a+b+c}{2}$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 11-3: Area of a Trapezoid

> The area of a **trapezoid** with bases $b_1$, $b_2$ and height $h$:

$$A = \frac{1}{2}h(b_1 + b_2)$$

**Example:** Bases 6 and 10, height 4:
$$A = \frac{1}{2}(4)(6 + 10) = \frac{1}{2}(4)(16) = 32$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 11-4: Area of a Rhombus or Kite

> The area equals **half the product of the diagonals**.

$$A = \frac{1}{2}d_1 d_2$$

**Example:** Rhombus with diagonals 8 and 6:
$$A = \frac{1}{2}(8)(6) = 24$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Regular Polygon — Key Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Center** | Center of the circumscribed circle |
| **Apothem ($a$)** | Perpendicular distance from center to a side |
| **Central angle** | $\dfrac{360°}{n}$ for a regular $n$-gon |

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 11-5: Area of a Regular Polygon

> The area of a **regular polygon** with perimeter $P$ and apothem $a$:

$$A = \frac{1}{2}Pa$$

**Example:** Regular hexagon with side 6:
- $P = 36$, apothem $= 3\sqrt{3}$
- $A = \frac{1}{2}(36)(3\sqrt{3}) = 54\sqrt{3} \approx 93.5$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 11-6: Circumference of a Circle

$$C = 2\pi r = \pi d$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Theorem 11-7: Area of a Circle

$$A = \pi r^2$$

**Example:** Radius $= 5$:
$$A = \pi (5)^2 = 25\pi \approx 78.5$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Arc Length

> The length of an arc with measure $x°$ in a circle of radius $r$:

$$\ell = \frac{x}{360} \cdot 2\pi r$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Area of a Sector

> The area of a **sector** with central angle $x°$ in a circle of radius $r$:

$$A = \frac{x}{360} \cdot \pi r^2$$

---

<!-- _class: example -->

### Example — Sector Area

A circle has radius 10. Find the area of a sector with central angle $72°$.

$$A = \frac{72}{360} \cdot \pi (10)^2 = \frac{1}{5} \cdot 100\pi = 20\pi \approx 62.8$$

---

<!-- _class: theorem -->

## Geometric Probability — Length/Area

> **Geometric probability** = ratio of favorable measure to total measure.

$$P = \frac{\text{favorable length or area}}{\text{total length or area}}$$

**Example:** Point chosen randomly inside a 10×10 square; circle of radius 3 inside. Probability of landing in circle:
$$P = \frac{\pi(3)^2}{100} = \frac{9\pi}{100} \approx 28.3\%$$
